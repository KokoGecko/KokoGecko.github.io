KokoGecko

Alice sends single photons to Bob, each in a random basis. Make the gecko jump into the fibre and it reads them on the way. Every photon it measures in the wrong basis arrives disturbed, so the error rate Bob sees climbs toward 25%. That rise is how quantum key distribution catches an eavesdropper.

Arrow keys or A / D to walk, space to jump, drag the sphere to turn it.
